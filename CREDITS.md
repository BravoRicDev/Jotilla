[Jotilla repository](https://github.com/GarauGarau/Jotilla), MIT license.
Screenshots and demos show the Windows application, not a simulated interface.

The page background is a resized WebP copy of the same museum photograph already
used by the app's documentation capture script. The screenshots were converted
to WebP, and the original GIF recordings to MP4, without changing app content.
System icons are small inline vector drawings in the site's own monochrome style.
No remotely hosted media, fonts, embeds or analytics are loaded by the page.
